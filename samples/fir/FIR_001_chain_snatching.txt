12. First Information contents:

I, Kavitha Raghunath, residing at the above address, state that on 14/03/2025 at about
8:45 pm I was returning home on foot after buying vegetables from the market on 12th Main
Road. When I reached near Sri Lakshmi Provision Store, a black scooter came slowly from behind
with two young men on it. The person sitting at the back suddenly pulled the gold mangalsutra
chain from my neck. I tried to hold the chain and fell down on the road and hurt my left elbow.
The scooter then went very fast towards 100 Feet Road.

The person who snatched the chain was not wearing a helmet. He was thin and was wearing a grey
jacket with a hood. The person driving the scooter wore a red helmet. I could only see the last
digits of the number plate as 7781 and that it started with KA-03.

The shopkeeper of Sri Lakshmi Provision Store, Sri Manjunath, came running when I shouted and
helped me get up. He also saw the scooter going away. After this I went home and told my husband,
and then we came to the police station.

I request you to take action against the unknown persons and recover my chain.

Signature of complainant: (signed) Kavitha R.
