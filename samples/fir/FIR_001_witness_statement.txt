STATEMENT OF WITNESS
Recorded at Indiranagar Police Station on 15/03/2025 at 11:30 hrs
In connection with FIR No. 0412/2025

Name: Manjunath Gowda, age 41, proprietor of Sri Lakshmi Provision Store, 12th Main Road,
HAL 2nd Stage, Indiranagar.

I run Sri Lakshmi Provision Store on 12th Main Road. On Friday 14th March, at around 8:30 or
8:40 in the night, I was closing the accounts at the counter. I heard a lady shouting "chain,
chain" outside. I ran out and saw Smt. Kavitha madam lying on the road near my shop. Her elbow
was bleeding.

I saw a black Dio scooter going very fast towards 100 Feet Road with two boys. The one at the
back was not wearing a helmet and had a grey jacket. I have seen that same scooter parked near
the tea stall at the 13th Main junction two or three times in the last week, in the evenings.
The same two boys used to stand there smoking.

I did not see the number plate. I helped madam to get up and gave her water. She then went home.

I can identify the boy without helmet if I see him again.

Signature: (signed) Manjunath G.
